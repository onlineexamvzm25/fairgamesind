How to play SETS

Pick one card from STOCK or OPEN, then discard one card. Build exactly three valid groups using your nine cards.

  • Three cards of the same rank.
  • Two cards of the same rank plus one printed joker.
  • One card plus two printed jokers.

Each player sees their own private hand. Pick one card, then discard one card, or submit a declaration.
